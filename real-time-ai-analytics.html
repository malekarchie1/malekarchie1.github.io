How Real-Time AI Analytics Change In-Game Decision Making

Published: March 1, 2026

Artificial intelligence is transforming the way coaches make decisions during basketball games. By tracking player movement, shot selection, and defensive positioning in real time, AI provides actionable insights that were previously impossible to gather quickly.

Coaches can use this information to:

  • Make smarter substitutions based on performance metrics
  • Adjust defensive and offensive strategies dynamically
  • Optimize shot selection and spacing for offensive efficiency

This differs from traditional statistics, which only provide post-game insights. Real-time AI analytics allow for immediate adjustments and better player development decisions throughout the game.
