AI Tools & Systems for Basketball

Here are some real tools and platforms that use AI and tracking to help players, coaches, and teams analyze performance and improve skills.

Shot Tracking & Analytics

  • ShotTracker – A system for capturing player movement, shot accuracy, and advanced analytics in real time.
  • Noah Basketball – Precision shot tracking and feedback used by NCAA and professional programs.

AI Film Study & Performance Platforms

  • HoopVision AI – AI video analysis for performance and shot feedback.
  • WizShot AI – AI-powered shooting analysis and mechanics improvement tools.

Wearables & Load Management (Examples)

Wearable systems like Catapult and PlayerTek are commonly used in high-performance settings to monitor workload, jump metrics, and recovery. Specific implementations vary by team and program.
