Wearables & Load Management (General Examples)

Wearable tech and load monitoring systems (e.g., Catapult, PlayerTek) are frequently used in high‑performance settings, though specific implementations vary by team and sport. These tools use sensors to track movement, workload, and fatigue — helping inform recovery decisions and prevent injury.
