About the Newsletter

Applied AI in Basketball focuses on how artificial intelligence is being used in real basketball settings. This includes training technology, film study tools, wearables, and recovery systems that teams and players are starting to rely on more each season.

About Me

My name is Malek Archie, and I’m a college student and student-athlete. I’m interested in how technology is changing sports, especially basketball. I chose this topic because AI is becoming more common in athletics, and I want to understand how it affects performance, preparation, and recovery.
