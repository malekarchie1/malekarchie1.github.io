AI at the Olympics: The New Standard for Performance Tracking

February 8, 2026

Watching the Winter Olympics this week, it is clear that sports technology has entered a new era. Companies like Samsung and Google DeepMind have introduced AI-driven systems that track athlete movements in real-time with incredible precision.

For any athlete, the "feel" of a performance is important, but it isn't always accurate. Traditionally, we have had to rely on coaches reviewing film hours after a session to find mistakes. Now, these AI vision tools can calculate exact rotation speeds and airtime the moment a competitor leaves the ramp.

While this level of data is impressive, it also changes the pressure of the game. Having a computer instantly identify why a move wasn't perfect adds a new layer of mental challenge. It will be interesting to see how quickly this technology moves from the Olympic stage into collegiate weight rooms and practice fields.
