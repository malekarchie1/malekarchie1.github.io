AI Wearables Are Becoming a Normal Part of Basketball Training

January 25, 2026

Wearable technology has moved way beyond just counting steps. In basketball, AI-powered wearables like smart jerseys, compression sleeves, and sensor-equipped shoes are now tracking everything from heart rate and jump height to specific muscle fatigue levels.

The AI inside these devices analyzes the movement data to tell players when they are at a high risk for injury or when they need to adjust their landing mechanics. It’s like having a personal trainer watching every single move you make on the court.

As the technology gets smaller and more affordable, these tools are trickling down from the pros to high school and college programs. For players, it means fewer injuries and a much better understanding of how their bodies handle the stress of a long season.
